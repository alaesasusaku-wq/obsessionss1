miraculous

Miraculous: Tales of Ladybug & Cat Noir follows Parisian teenagers Marinette and Adrien, who transform into the superheroes Ladybug and Cat Noir to protect their city. They must stop the villain Hawk Moth, who secretly uses magical butterflies to turn emotionally vulnerable citizens into supervillains. Unknown to each other, Hawk Moth is actually Adrien’s father, Gabriel Agreste, who wants to steal the heroes' magical jewelry to make a reality-altering wish and resurrect his comatose wife. The story is driven by this tragic family conflict and an ironic romantic dynamic: Marinette loves Adrien but dislikes Cat Noir, while Adrien loves Ladybug but ignores Marinette.

Arcane: League of Legends

Arcane follows the tragic separation of two orphaned sisters, Vi and Powder, amidst a brewing war between the wealthy city of Piltover and its oppressed undercity, Zaun. While Vi fights alongside an enforcer to restore peace, Powder is adopted by a ruthless crime lord and transforms into the chaotic, weapon-wielding Jinx. Trapped by trauma and opposing technologies, the sisters' broken relationship ultimately triggers a devastating war between their worlds.

Naruto Shippuden

Naruto Shippuden follows a teenage Naruto Uzumaki as he reunites with Sakura Haruno after years of training, with both determined to rescue their rogue teammate, Sasuke Uchiha, from the path of darkness. As the three original Team 7 members grow into elite warriors, they must also defend the ninja world against the Akatsuki, a dangerous organization hunting Naruto for the fox demon sealed inside him. While Sakura develops into a master medical ninja with devastating strength, Naruto leads the global alliance into a massive war against ancient threats. Through tragic sacrifices and fierce battles, Naruto and Sakura ultimately help bring Sasuke back to the light, break the cycle of hatred, and save humanity.

Off Campus

The Off-Campus series by Elle Kennedy follows a tight-knit group of popular college hockey players at Briar University who each find unexpected love with women who challenge, support, and transform them. Starting with the fake-dating agreement between witty music major Hannah and arrogant team captain Garrett, the narrative expands across subsequent books to follow his teammates Logan, Dean, and Tucker as they navigate their own complex relationships. While the series is celebrated for its sharp banter, high-stakes sports drama, and intense romance, it also deeply explores heavier real-world themes like overcoming past trauma, family expectations, and the daunting transition into adulthood. Ultimately, the series is anchored by the group's fierce loyalty to one another, showing how their deep bonds of friendship help them heal and grow into their futures.

Avengers

The Avengers are a team of extraordinary superheroes who unite to protect Earth and the universe from major threats.Formed by Nick Fury and the spy agency S.H.I.E.L.D., the initial roster includes Iron Man, Captain America, Thor, Hulk, Black Widow, and Hawkeye. Over time, the group faces cosmic invasions, rogue artificial intelligence like Ultron, and internal political divides. Their ultimate test comes against the cosmic tyrant Thanos, who erases half of all life in the universe. The surviving heroes eventually regroup, travel through time to undo the devastation, and defeat Thanos in a final, massive battle to restore the universe.

Never have I ever

Created by Mindy Kaling and Lang Fisher, Never Have I Ever is a coming-of-age comedy-drama on Netflix that follows Devi Vishwakumar (Maitreyi Ramakrishnan), a hot-tempered, ambitious 15-year-old first-generation Indian-American high school sophomore living in Sherman Oaks, California.Coping with the sudden, traumatic death of her father Mohan—which temporarily psychogenically paralyzed her—Devi is determined to elevate her social standing, shed her nerdy label, and land a handsome boyfriend, navigating a chaotic love triangle between jock Paxton Hall-Yoshida and academic rival Ben Gross. Across four seasons, the series humorously and tenderly explores themes of grief, cultural identity, teenage angst, and the repairing of a complex, loving relationship with her strict mother, Nalini.

Sterling Point

Sterling Point is a coming-of-age mystery drama series on Prime Video that follows 17-year-old Type-A New Yorker Annie Jacobson and her twin brother Connor, who unexpectedly inherit a private Canadian island in Lake Muskoka from their estranged, late grandfather. Driven by the rejection from her dream summer program and the discovery of hidden letters, Annie takes a solo trip to the island only to find the family cabin occupied by Ramona, a hostile stranger who turns out to be the biological daughter of the twins' late adoptive mother, Goldie. As Annie navigates the local community, a dramatic summer love triangle, and intense class divides, she and Ramona gradually form a deep bond while digging into their parents' messy history. The season reaches a climax when a genetic realization about colorblindness prompts a DNA test, revealing that Annie's adoptive father, Steven, is actually Ramona's biological father—unraveling a web of secrets regarding Goldie's past affair with a local man named Joe and her tragic death. By the end of the season, the siblings reach a truce regarding the property, setting up their futures as Ramona moves to New York to bond with Steven, while Annie stays behind to embrace life on the island.

Euphoria

Euphoria is a raw and hyper-stylized HBO drama series that follows a group of high school students navigating a chaotic landscape of love, trauma, identity, and addiction. The story centers on Rue Bennett, a 17-year-old recovering drug addict struggling to find her footing and stay clean after her father’s death, whose life changes dramatically when she forms a turbulent, codependent relationship with Jules Vaughn, a transgender newcomer to town. Around them, the series explores the deeply troubled lives of their peers—including Nate's toxic masculinity, Maddy's abusive relationships, Cassie's desperate need for validation, and Kat's exploration of body image—using surreal, neon-soaked visuals and intense emotional drama to capture the crushing anxieties of modern youth.

Ginny & Georgia

Ginny & Georgia is a Netflix comedy-drama television series that follows 31-year-old free-spirited mother Georgia Miller and her two children, Ginny and Austin, as they relocate to a picturesque New England town in Massachusetts for a fresh start.While Georgia tries to give her kids a normal life, her dark criminal past, complex secrets, and protective instincts continuously threaten their new stability. Stranger Things

Set in the 1980s in the fictional town of Hawkins, Indiana, the Netflix sci-fi series Stranger Things follows a tight-knit group of kids who uncover terrifying supernatural forces and government conspiracies after their friend Will Byers mysteriously vanishes. As they search for Will, they befriend a powerful telekinetic girl named Eleven who escaped from a secret local laboratory, leading them to battle horrific creatures from a dark alternate dimension known as the Upside Down. Across multiple seasons, Stranger Things Wiki characters including Will's mother Joyce, police chief Jim Hopper, and the growing group of teenagers and kids must repeatedly unite to fight escalating interdimensional threats—culminating in an epic struggle against the entity Vecna to save their hometown and the world.

The Babysitter: Killer Queen

The Babysitter: Killer Queen is a 2020 American horror-comedy film directed by McG. Two years after defeating his babysitter Bee's satanic blood cult, high schooler Cole Johnson continues to suffer from severe trauma while his parents and peers remain entirely convinced that he is mentally ill. During a weekend getaway at a lake party hosted by his friend and neighbor Melanie, Cole is horrified when his resurrected cult tormentors—including Max, Allison, Sonya, and John—unexpectedly return alongside Melanie, who reveals herself to be a new cult member. Forced to flee into the wilderness with a troubled new classmate named Phoebe, Cole must use his wits to survive the night and complete stranger tasks to stop the cult from sacrificing him. In a dramatic final showdown, the ritual ultimately fails and the cult members disintegrate because Cole is no longer a virgin after sleeping with Phoebe, leading Bee to emerge as the mastermind who orchestrated the entire setup to free both teens from their dark fates.

Death of a Unicorn

The A24 dark comedy horror film Death of a Unicorn follows a widowed lawyer, Elliot Kintner, and his estranged teenage daughter, Ridley, who accidentally hit a mystical baby unicorn with their car while traveling to a remote Canadian Rockies retreat. Hoping to secure a promotion, Elliot brings the creature's corpse to the compound of his billionaire boss, Odell Leopold, the head of a corrupt pharmaceutical empire who is dying of cancer. When the Leopolds discover that the unicorn's blood and horn possess supernatural, highly valuable regenerative healing properties, corporate greed takes over, and they immediately try to monetize the rare creature to cure Odell. However, their attempts at ruthless exploitation trigger a gory catastrophe when the foal's massive, monstrous parent unicorns arrive at the compound, brutally slaughtering the billionaire family and their staff in an act of violent vengeance.

Spider-Man: Brand New Day

Set four years after the memory-erasing events of No Way Home, Spider-Man: Brand New Day follows an adult Peter Parker living in deep isolation as a full-time, street-level superhero in New York City. While quietly keeping tabs on MJ and Ned's lives via social media, Peter combats local threats alongside NYPD Detective Jean DeWolff, successfully taking down major criminal forces like Mac Gargan and the Hand ninjas. However, the immense emotional grief and physical strain of his lonely lifestyle trigger a painful physical evolution and severe sensory overload, which severely impacts his control over his powers. This dangerous vulnerability hits exactly as a terrifying new psychic threat emerges: a powerful mutant named Gene Gray who uses telepathy and mental possession to terrorize New Yorkers. Forced into unexpected alliances with gritty figures like Frank Castle, Peter must ultimately overcome his profound loneliness, embrace his dual identity, and use radical empathy rather than physical violence to defeat the unseen enemy and protect a world that no longer remembers him.

Alien Stage

Alien Stage is a dystopian Korean animated web series created by VIVINOS and QMENG where human pets are forced to perform in a deadly, televised singing competition for the entertainment of alien overlords. Raised and trained alongside one another in the restrictive Anakt Garden facility, the main characters are pitted against each other in brutal tournament rounds where the loser is executed on stage. The narrative unfolds primarily through emotional music videos that highlight the tragic, fractured bonds, hidden affections, and desperate survival struggles of contestants like Mizi, Sua, Till, and Ivan as they navigate a hopeless system under alien rule.

The Summer I Turned Pretty

The Summer I Turned Pretty by Jenny Han is a coming-of-age young adult novel about a teenage girl named Isabel "Belly" Conklin, who measures her life in summers spent at Cousins Beach with her family and their close family friends, Susannah Fisher and her two sons, Conrad and Jeremiah. During the summer she turns sixteen, Belly finally feels pretty and undergoes a transformative shift, catching the attention of the Fisher brothers and complicating her long-standing, deep crush on the moody Conrad while navigating new dynamics with the more open and attentive Jeremiah. As the summer progresses, underlying family tensions surface when it is revealed that Susannah's cancer has returned, shifting the focus from teenage romance to collective grief, emotional maturity, and support. The novel concludes with poignant moments of heartbreak and clarity, including a shared kiss with Conrad that hints at future romance and a lingering sense of transition from childhood into the complexities of adolescence.

Culpa Nuestra

Culpa Nuestra (Our Fault), the dramatic conclusion to Mercedes Ron’s popular Culpables trilogy, follows Nick and Noah as they navigate the aftermath of their painful breakup. After an intentional separation, the estranged former lovers are forced to cross paths again at the wedding of their best friends, Jenna and Lion. Despite building separate lives—with Nick managing his family's massive business empire and Noah focusing on her new corporate career—their unresolved feelings and intense connection quickly resurface. To finally heal and discover if they are truly meant to be together, the two must confront lingering family obligations, personal trauma, and deep-seated resentments. Billie Eilish

Billie Eilish is a critically acclaimed American singer and songwriter who skyrocketed to global fame in 2015 with her debut single "Ocean Eyes." Born and raised in Los Angeles, California, she collaborates closely with her brother, Finneas O'Connell, to create a distinct, genre-defying sound that blends dark pop, electropop, and indie influences. Known for her signature whispered vocals, introspective lyrics, and unique aesthetic, Eilish made history as the youngest artist to win all four major Grammy categories in a single year. She has since solidified her status as a defining cultural icon of her generation, earning multiple Academy Awards for her cinematic songwriting alongside numerous chart-topping albums.
